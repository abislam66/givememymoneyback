How it works

  1. 1

    Add your bill and your EOB

    Upload a PDF or a photo of each, or type the numbers in. You check what we read before anything is decided.

  2. 2

    We compare every line

    Fixed rules, not guesses, match each charge to what your insurer says. They look for charges billed twice, missing insurance payments, price differences and surprise out-of-network bills.

  3. 3

    Understand it and act

    See each problem with its proof and math, ask questions in plain English, hear answers read aloud, and send a ready-made dispute letter.

Why you can trust it

Rules decide, not AI

Every problem comes from a rule in code, with its evidence. AI only reads your papers and rewords explanations, and its numbers are checked.

Your files aren't kept

Uploaded files are not permanently stored. The numbers you confirm and your results may be saved to your account, and only you can see them.

Honest about money

What we find is potential savings. It counts as saved only after your dispute is resolved.
